## A badge collapses the questions that matter

A single positive label cannot tell a reviewer which requirements were assessed, which source version was used, whether a value came from an original document or a later assertion, and which exceptions remain unresolved.

Readiness is more useful when it is specific. For each requirement, the record should expose the selected source basis, applicability result, evidence location, current claim, review decision, and remaining blocker.

## Missing is a productive state

When a team can see that evidence is missing—and who owns the next action—it can move. When uncertainty is averaged into a score, deadline risk becomes harder to locate.

The same applies to conflicts and staleness. These are not edge cases to hide. They are the work.

## A package should have a date and a boundary

Sources change. Products change. Organizations change. A portable package therefore needs a basis date, source and control versions, an entity boundary, decisions, and unresolved exceptions.

That record does not replace accountable judgment. It gives the accountable people something concrete to review.
