## Launch position

The v1 marketing site is designed without non-essential cookies. If cookieless analytics is configured, it does not use cookies or collect form content.

The colour-theme preference is stored locally in your browser and is used only to remember the display option you chose.

## Future changes

If a future scheduler, media embed, or measurement tool needs non-essential cookies, it will be gated behind a granular choice with rejection as the default. This notice and the sub-processor list must be updated before that tool loads.
