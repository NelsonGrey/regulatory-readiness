## In discovery

This pack is research only. It is not available for production preparation, and no delivery date is promised.

It exists to prove a point about the engine: a regulation with entirely different entities, facts, and applicability logic — battery categories, capacity thresholds, recycled-content and due-diligence duties, state-of-health data — runs through the same workspace, evidence requests, review gates, and export as the accessibility pack, with no regulation-specific application code.

Source coverage, applicability rules, and two-person review are not complete. When they are, this card changes to **available**.
