## The deadline is already in force

Ireland's implementing regulations came into operation on **28 June 2025**. The rules cover specified consumer products and services, with scope, transitional arrangements, and exceptions that depend on the organization, offering, and circumstances.

The workspace does not decide legal scope for you. It records the facts needed for an applicability decision, shows conditional areas, and gives your adviser or accountable owner a structured basis for review.

## Who may be in scope

Covered areas include certain consumer computing hardware, self-service terminals, electronic communications, audiovisual media access, passenger transport information and ticketing, consumer banking, e-books, and e-commerce. Product and service duties differ. Microenterprise treatment also differs between products and services.

Start with the actual entity and offering. A category label alone is not enough to reach a defensible conclusion.

## What readiness means here

For each applicable requirement, the package distinguishes:

- evidence accepted against the chosen control snapshot;
- missing evidence and its assigned owner;
- competing assertions that still require a decision;
- evidence that may be stale because its source or subject changed;
- conditional controls awaiting an entity fact; and
- requirements that are not yet required under the selected basis.

Where ICT conformance evidence is relevant, the pack maps the working matrix to **EN 301 549 V3.2.1** and its referenced **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** criteria. That mapping is a preparation aid, not a substitute for specialist testing or legal analysis.

## What you produce with this pack

- A scoped entity and applicability-facts record.
- An evidence matrix against the selected EN 301 549 / WCAG basis.
- A report of missing, conflicting, stale, and conditional items.
- An accessibility-statement draft with a source-linked evidence index.
- A dated export package with control, source, claim, and decision versions.

## What this does not do

It does not certify an organization or offering, guarantee conformity, provide a legal opinion, decide whether an exception applies, or perform specialist lab and metric work unless separately scoped with a qualified provider.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this only a checklist?

No. The pack is a versioned control catalog joined to entity facts, applicability rules, evidence locations, review decisions, contradiction handling, and a dated export. The list of requirements is only one part of the record.

### Can an automated extractor approve our documents?

No. Extraction may create a proposal. A person must accept or reject it, and a proposal without a stable source location cannot be accepted as a claim.

### Does this replace an accessibility audit or legal adviser?

No. It organizes the inputs, gaps, provenance, and decisions those specialists need. Specialist testing and legal judgment remain separate services.

### What if we claim disproportionate burden or fundamental alteration?

The platform can preserve your inputs and decision record. It does not decide that an exception applies. That judgment stays with the responsible organization and its advisers.
